Overview

Indonesian folklore, once a vital medium for passing down moral lessons, has faced a significant decline due to its struggle to transition from oral traditions into the modern digital landscape. Consequently, many of these stories remain confined to specific regions, making them difficult for wider audiences to access or preserve in the contemporary world.

Sketches

We will create products that raise awareness about folklores and serves as a medium of self-expression by connecting customer’s personalities with folklore characters through folklore inspired designs. The sketches are made based on 8 different regions in Indonesia with different folklore and clothing.

Branding

KathaRakyat is a brand that is on a mission to bring Indonesian tradition folktales to the modern world! We provide trendy trinkets filled with blind boxes, art prints, and cute stickers. With this we aim to retell folklore stories from different Region in Indonesia to the younger generations.
