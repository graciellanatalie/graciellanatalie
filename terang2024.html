Overview

Terang 2024 is a projection mapping competition held by wearefilamen in Matic, Kuala Lumpur. This competition carried the theme Cultural Futurism, which explores the fusion of Malaysia’s tradition and technology.

Creative Ideation

Flying through a journey exploring the beauty of Malaysia, traversing the dimensions of time and culture. Audiences will explore the rich heritage of Malaysia, seamlessly blending the traditional elements of Malay, Chinese, and Indian cultures. Through harmonious visuals and sound, we weave a tapestry that connects the past to the future.

Sketches

By fusing art, technology, and innovation, we offer a glimpse into how Malaysia’s diverse cultural roots continue to evolve and flourish in a futuristic landscape.

Storyboard

The storyboard is developed based on research and development with scenes detail including notes, time, sfx, vfx, and gfx.

Character Design

The name Azahra, has the meaning of "radiant" or “shining.”

The outfit she wears is a tiger costume that is inspired by Malaysia’s representative animal, Malayan Tiger. Her outfit which consist of a Poncho and skirt are both inspired by Malaysian traditional clothes. Azahra's skirt is inspired by the traditional baju Kurung. She also wears the signature sandal.

This name was chosen because we hope that Azahra can be a light bringing Malaysia’s many cultures to the futuristic era.

3D Modelling Assets

3D Building is made based on the real building to capture transition in between scenes.

Animatic

This journey explores Malaysia’s cultural evolution through four phases—Malay, Chinese, Indian, and Futuristic—using traditional batik, art, and vibrant patterns to celebrate a rich heritage. Guided by Azahra, the experience culminates in a vision of a harmonious future where diverse ethnicities coexist and flourish within an evolving landscape.

Motion Graphics

Scenes for build up and cultural combination where the Wau Bulan finished going through all scenarios.
