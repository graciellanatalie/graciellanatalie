Overview

A projection mapping competition held in Laswi, bandung. This competition carried the them of Looking After Dark, emphasizing that behind the bright lights of the city, there are spaces left in shadow, both literally and metaphorically.

Creative Ideation

The research focused on the dual causes of urban darkness: the environmental impact of light pollution and the physical accumulation of electronic waste. This phase established a visual language where 'the dark' is represented by the discarded tech that shadows our modern cities.

Storyboard

The narrative follows the last small star—a final beacon of hope—on a quest to revive light after a goddess's energy is consumed by darkness. The journey transitions through arcade-inspired challenges and industrial factories, culminating in a battle to reclaim energy from the manifestations of e-waste

2D Modelling Assets

Ominous eye motifs that hunt for remaining light—alongside detailed factory silhouettes to illustrate industrial waste. These are complemented by blinking star VFX to add layers of movement and depth to the projection's atmosphere.

3D Modelling Assets

Utilizing Blender, I modeled vintage televisions to personify the 'evil' entity of e-waste. To contrast this, I created high-energy arcade assets—including slot machines, podiums, and coin machines—to represent the lively, pulsating energy of light and hope.

Animatic

The animatic served as a technical blueprint to time the star’s journey through the arcade and factory environments. This allowed for precise synchronization between the narrative beats and the architectural features of the projection site.

Music Production

The soundscape is driven by high-energy EDM to capture the intense conflict between light and dark. I integrated arcade SFX and glitchy electronic textures to reinforce the gaming-inspired visuals and the project's technological themes
