Overview

PLATOF (Platform of the Future) is a branding and design project about a futuristic yet daily-wearable platform shoe. The project involved the footwear and a 3x5m pop-up booth. The final design included 3D-rendered environments for the product photoshoots and a booth design with real-life measurements for physical production.

Branding

The brand identity is inspired by a luminous star and the edge of a shoe. The shoes are designed to be modular and futuristic but still comfortable.

3D Modelling

The modelling of the shoe is made from scratch to capture the mecha look of a shoe with customizable decorations.

Floorpan

The layout is made to ensure foot trafic is clean and that the products are displayed nicely.

Booth Elements

Booth elements includes the moodular booth, chairs, tables, shelfs, cashier table, and totem.

Booth Design

The booth design is inspired by a spaceship interior, utilizing a flow strategy to manage foot traffic and was designed with a modular structure to ensure the store could be easily relocated.
