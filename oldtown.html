Overview

The Jakarta Light Festival is one of the events in a series of activities commemorating Jakarta's 497th anniversary. This event includes a Video Mapping Competition, “Malam Jaya Raya”, that highlights the theme of Jakarta's history.

Creative Ideation

In a world rich with stories and meaning, the city of Jakarta presents a breathtaking narrative. Through its symbolic "red thread," Bhaskara weaves through the history of Batavia, Sunda Kelapa, and the modern Jakarta Metropolitan, aiming to transport the audience across dimensions of space and time. This visual journey, accompanied by harmonious music, showcases the ties binding the past to the present—a fusion of Jakarta’s cultural arts and the diverse ethnicities of our beloved Indonesia.

Sketches

Bhaskara is sketched inspired heavily by Ondel-ondel Betawi with colors inspired by the traditional culture outfits.

Character Design

Derived from Old Javanese, the name Bhaskara means "Sun" or "Giver of Light." It originates from the Sanskrit word भास (Bhasa), which translates to "radiance" or "glow." We chose this name with the hope that our mascot, Bhaskara, would serve as a brilliant light—guiding the audience through this journey and radiating positive energy much like the sun itself.

Motion Graphics

Our animation follows Bhaskara’s journey through three phases—History, Connection, and Development—using a symbolic "red thread" to weave Jakarta’s evolution from its ancient origins into a unified, modern metropolis. Inspired by the vibrant colors and patterns of Batik Betawi, the visual style shifts from primary tones to a diverse palette to represent the flourishing growth and shared hope of the city’s people.
