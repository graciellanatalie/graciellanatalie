Overview

Ashita Nova follows the story of a lonely spirit who regained her hope of a warm and joyous new year celebration through the help of her friends, the fireflies. Once again painting the sky with her vibrant colors, marking a new beginning.

Storyboard

The story follows Nova, the Spirit of the New Year, as she transforms a melancholic, silent city by gathering fragmented human hopes that appear as glowing fireflies. Her joyful dance culminates in a spectacular explosion of light where the fireflies morph into vibrant fireworks, illuminating the sky with a message of "Happy New Year" for a fresh beginning.

Character Design

Originating from Latin, the name Nova means “new” or “new star”.Her name symbolizes a new beginning as the embodiement of the new year spirit herself. Her outfits resembles the new year bells and the night sky of New Year with a candle lit on her head symbolizing a new year.

Character 3D Modelling

Character is then modelled in Blender with proper topology for rigging purposes in Motion Capture.

Character Texturing

The 2 different texture shows the difference when Nova has not gotten the new year spirit back, and after when the new year energy is back.

Additional 3D Modelling

Additional 3D models are made to fulfill encironment purposes including buildings, bench, log, and etc.

Environment Design

The environment is made to create that natural look of the hills with moving grass that is textured like it is handpainted.

Music Production

The music is made to capture the sadness in the beginning and the build up of hope towards a happy ending. There are SFX that also becomes part of Nova's movements.
