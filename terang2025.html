Overview

Terang 2025 is a projection mapping competition held by wearefilamen in George Town, Penang. This competition carried the theme “Rojak”, which explores cultural identity of Malaysia.

Creative Ideation

Malaysia is formed from a fusion of many cultures: Indian, Chinese, and Malay. Using this as the foundation for the story of this project, we aim to blend these cultures with the theme of this competition, which is Rojak.

Sketches

All civilization characters are inspired by native animals from Malaysia, China, and India, reflecting the country’s three largest ethnic groups. Storyboard is sketched according to the storyline agreed.

2D Assets

In charge of some 2D assets including diwali, peranakan tiles, and villain minions they are created based on Research and Development.

3D Modelling

To highlight each kingdom’s cultural identity, the architecture combines elements from Indian, Malay, and Chinese traditions. Through this visual, we aim to show that while the three kingdoms coexisted in harmony, true unity had yet to be achieved.

To reach the ending they’ve been fighting for, those three kingdoms must face obstacles created by the villain. This building is inspired by geometric stumbling blocks to represent the hindrance.

Animatic

Three diverse kingdoms once lived in distant peace until a rising darkness forced them to unite and discover a strength far greater than their own. Through this unity, they realized their enemy was merely a misunderstood soul, ultimately replacing hatred with the harmony of true belonging.

Music Production

The music is inspired by the combination of traditional Chinese, Indian, and Malaysian songs and instruments.
