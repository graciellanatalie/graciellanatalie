Frequently Asked Question

What is your typical project timeline?

Every project is unique, but a standard 3D or motion project usually takes between 1 to 2 weeks from initial concept to final delivery. For more complex technical tools or large-scale projects, we can discuss a custom roadmap.

How do we get started on a project?

It starts with a brief. Once we align on the goals, I move into the concept and blocking phase. You’ll receive updates at every major milestone to ensure the vision is on track.

What software and tools do you use?

My primary software includes Blender for 3D projects, the Adobe Creative Suite (After Effects, Substance Painter, Illustrator) for textures and motion, and HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python for coding related project.
